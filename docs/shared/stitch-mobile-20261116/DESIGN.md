# نوادر الإبل — Accessible Arabic Mobile Design
Side MOBILE. Task MOBILE-GUI-STITCH-20261116. Scope new Stitch mobile project and docs/shared delivery only. Follow SRS 4.1-draft sections 3–8. Acceptance: complete mobile journey coverage, simple RTL UI, authentic attached logo, exact source color tokens, readable accessible controls; design only.
## Audience and tone
Saudi Arabic camel classifieds. Designed for older adults and people unfamiliar with technology. Plain Saudi Arabic labels. One clear primary action per screen. No unnecessary decoration or crowded grids. Large single-column listing cards. 56px buttons and 48px minimum tap targets, generous 20px margins, 12px radii. Noto Sans Arabic (load Google Fonts) with 18px body/labels, 24–28px headings, 14px minimum supplementary text. Clear black/dark text. Bottom navigation right-to-left: الرئيسية، المفضلة، أضف إعلان، الرسائل، حسابي. All icons have visible words. Bottom safe area, sticky action above safe area. Right-side back chevron.

## Functional rules
Guest browsing without login. Phone and OTP are LTR islands in RTL. No invented OTP length or cooldown; shown value comes from settings. Consents initially unchecked. Login phone private; contact number separate, call and/or WhatsApp plus explicit publication consent required.
Classified ads for ONE camel. Fixed price OR in-person auction; no electronic bids, checkout, deposits, timers or winner. Fixed price positive SAR. Auction future date/time Saudi timezone, venue and published map point; show المزايدة حضورية خارج التطبيق. Past auction state مضى موعد المزاد without winner.
Creation in small steps with progress and back/save draft: type, camel data, condition/description, region/city/location, type-specific price or auction venue/date, photos/video, contact consents, preview and declaration. Region changes clear city. Photo default 3–10, max 10MB each, JPEG/PNG; video optional 60s/100MB; configurable limits. Real photo placeholders with no private data.
Draft/review/rejected/published/hidden/archived owner states. Editing published ad hides it ONLY after submitting revision for review, not while drafting. Bump after minimum 24h; eligibility/cooldown from server, not competitive expiry. Archive confirmation; resubmit archived via review, hidden requires admin.
Search region/city/breed/sex/offer type/age in months/price; filter and sort visibly different. Price filter excludes auctions, auction never zero-price. Map has manual center/radius and list fallback; priced ad approximate point optional, auction precise venue with consent.
Favorites unavailable cards reveal no hidden text/media. Public advertiser profile with distinct هاتف مؤكد/هوية موثقة/نشاط موثق, join date and ratings; not verified purchase. Saved searches/follows can stop. Notifications no private message previews.
Private text chat send/pending/failed/retry/read; blocked/hidden/suspended is read-only; no hidden ad content header. Public questions edit/delete own, seller reply/close questions, report abuse. Block confirmation and unblock.
Rating 1–5 text only after reciprocal chat, active phone-confirmed account and unchecked declaration تعاملت مع هذا المعلن; no self/duplicate; editable, seller reply and appeal does not auto-hide. Reports private reference/status.
Identity verification trusted external provider, business private document upload; draft/submitted/checking/approved/rejected/expired/cancelled; unavailable provider must not award badge.
Account edit/notifications/follows/blocked/privacy contact withdrawal/logout/close account confirmation. Important in-app notifications persist when push denied. Loading/empty/error/offline/permission denied/upload failure/rate limited/suspended/unavailable states with clear recovery, preserve correct inputs.
